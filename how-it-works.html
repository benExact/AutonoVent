How It Works

Autonovent is designed to help keep dust out of your canopy by automatically responding to your driving conditions and the weather. No manual adjustments needed.

How Autonovent works

Smart airflow.
Automatic protection.

Drive. AutonoVent opens.

When your vehicle starts moving, AutonoVent automatically opens its front vent to let air flow into the canopy. This airflow helps create negative pressure inside the canopy, reducing the amount of dust that enters your load space.

Rain detected. Vent closed.

When rain or moisture is detected near the vent, AutonoVent automatically closes to help protect your canopy from the elements. This does not make the canopy waterproof.

Stop. AutonoVent closes.

When your vehicle stops, the vent automatically closes, ready for your next journey.

Long-lasting power. No sunny parking spot required.

AutonoVent has a built-in battery designed to power the unit for over a year without sunlight. You can park in the shade, in a garage or out of direct sunlight without needing to find a sunny spot just to charge it.

Less dust. Less hassle. More confidence on the road.
