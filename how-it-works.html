How It Works

Autonovent uses airflow to help keep dust out of a vehicle canopy. The front opening is the air intake. Air is drawn out of the canopy through that intake, which helps create negative pressure inside.

The principle

On a dusty road, air moving around the vehicle can pull dust in through small gaps. Negative pressure inside the canopy works the other way: air wants to move out, so dust is less likely to be drawn in.

  • The unit mounts on the canopy roof, around the middle-front.
  • The front opening is the intake. Air from the canopy enters there.
  • Air then leaves the unit, so the flow is out of the canopy, not into it.
  • The result is intended to help reduce dust ingress. It is not a vacuum, and it does not make a canopy dustproof.
