Autonovent

Installation

Autonovent is a compact unit, about the size of an A4 sheet, designed to bolt onto a bakkie canopy around the middle-front of the roof. The front opening is the air intake and should face the direction of travel.

Where it mounts

The intended position is the canopy roof, around the middle and toward the front, with the front intake facing the direction of travel.
