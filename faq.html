FAQs

Straight answers from what is known about Autonovent. Where a detail has not been confirmed, the page says so.

  • What is Autonovent?

    A compact, self-contained air vent that mounts on a vehicle canopy. It is about the size of an A4 sheet.

  • What does it do?

    It draws air out of the canopy through the front intake. That outward flow helps create negative pressure inside, which can reduce dust ingress on dusty roads. It does not make a canopy dustproof.

  • Which way does the air move?

    The front opening is the intake. Air from inside the canopy enters there and leaves the unit. The flow is out of the canopy, not into it.

  • Where does it mount?

    Around the middle-front of the canopy roof, with the front intake facing the direction of travel. It is designed to bolt onto the canopy.
