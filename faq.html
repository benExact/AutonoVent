FAQs

Straight answers from what is known about Autonovent. Where a detail has not been confirmed, the page says so.

  • What is Autonovent?

    A compact, self-contained air vent that mounts on a vehicle canopy. It is about the size of an A4 sheet.

  • What does it do?

    It opens automatically while you drive so air can flow into the canopy. That airflow is designed to help create negative pressure and reduce dust in the load space. It closes when the vehicle stops, and when rain or moisture is detected. It does not make a canopy dust-free or waterproof.

  • Which way does the air move?

    While you drive, the front vent opens and air flows into the canopy. When the vehicle stops, or when rain or moisture is detected near the vent, it closes.

  • Where does it mount?

    Around the middle-front of the canopy roof, with the front intake facing the direction of travel. It is designed to bolt onto the canopy.

  • Will it fit my vehicle?

    A vehicle and canopy fitment list has not been published. The shop is where Autonovent is listed for purchase.

  • How much does it cost?

    A confirmed selling price is shown on the shop when it has been saved for the product. In local development, an unpriced product can show a labelled test price. That test price is not a confirmed selling price.

  • What warranty is included?
