Shipping and returns

Draft for review. This page does not set a delivery promise, a return deadline or a warranty period. Those decisions have not been confirmed. The Terms and Conditions (version v1.0-draft) are the longer statement.

Shipping

Delivery at checkout is limited to South Africa. The server calculates the delivery amount and shows it before you place the order. In the current development checkout that amount is a simulated test rate. It is not a courier price, and placing the order does not book a shipment.

A delivery estimate has not been confirmed. The checkout does not provide live parcel tracking. An email about payment or fulfilment status, when email is configured, is an order message and not a tracking service.

You need to provide a contact number and a complete address. Add a unit, apartment or complex name if the street address alone will not find you. Address suggestions can leave those details blank.

Returns, refunds and warranty

A change-of-mind return window has not been confirmed. A warranty duration has not been confirmed. A refund method for live payments has not been confirmed, because a live payment provider has not been appointed. Simulated checkout does not take real money, so a simulated payment is not refunded as cash.

If a product is defective or damaged, rights under the Consumer Protection Act still apply. The return address for sending a product back has not been confirmed. Contact the business once the contact details in the terms are published.
