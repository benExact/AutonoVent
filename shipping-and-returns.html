Shipping

Delivery at checkout is limited to South Africa. The delivery charge is calculated and shown before you pay. It is included once. Placing the order does not book a shipment.

A delivery estimate has not been confirmed. The checkout does not provide live parcel tracking. An email about payment or fulfilment status, when email is configured, is an order message and not a tracking service.

You need to provide a contact number and a complete address. Add a unit, apartment or complex name if the street address alone will not find you. Address suggestions can leave those details blank.
